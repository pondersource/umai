The Recipes, Movies and Flashcards modules own scope storage and read-only
imports of existing Solid data. Internal RDF identifiers are not public links.
Public sharing copies only selected documents; private originals stay private.

Google/Dropbox callback: this app's HTTPS origin without a trailing slash.
Google also needs the app origin registered as a JavaScript origin.
Google and Dropbox return to the home route; Solid restores the previous route.
OneDrive callback: this app's HTTPS origin plus /. Register it as a SPA redirect
URI in the shared Microsoft application before sign-in. Personal accounts only.
LWS uses its native OpenID sign-in and restores the app route after consent.
